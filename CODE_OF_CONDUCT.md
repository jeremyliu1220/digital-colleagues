# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We commit to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free project for everyone,
regardless of background, identity, experience, disability, appearance, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and evidence without attacking people.
- Give actionable, proportionate feedback and accept correction with care.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, attribution, and intellectual-property boundaries.
- Make space for newcomers and people with different expertise.
- Stop conduct that a moderator identifies as disruptive or unsafe.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing,
deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption, and retaliation are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in project repositories, reviews, discussions, events, and when a
person represents the project publicly. Report conduct privately through the repository
moderation or security-reporting mechanism described in `SECURITY.md`; do not publish
sensitive or identifying details.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue a warning, limit participation, temporarily
suspend access, or permanently exclude a participant. Decisions should consider impact,
pattern, safety, proportionality, and the privacy of everyone involved. A maintainer named
in a report must recuse from handling it. Retaliation against a reporter or participant in
a good-faith review is prohibited.
