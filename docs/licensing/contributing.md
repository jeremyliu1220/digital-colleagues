# Contributor Licensing Guidance

Project-authored contributions are accepted under Apache License 2.0 on an inbound-equals-
outbound basis. Unless a contributor explicitly and conspicuously marks a communication as
not a contribution, an intentional submission for inclusion is offered under the terms in
the root `LICENSE` file. P1 adopts neither a contributor license agreement nor a Developer
Certificate of Origin sign-off process.

Contributors must have the right to submit their work. Do not submit employer-owned,
client-owned, confidential, private-repository, generated, or third-party material unless
the applicable rights and obligations are documented and compatible. Repository access is
not proof of permission.

For third-party or generated material, record origin, exact version, license, applicable
notice, modification status, and distribution treatment before inclusion. Preserve
required notices. If ownership, attribution, patent, privacy, or relicensing scope is
uncertain, leave the material out and add it to the unresolved rights inventory.

Follow `docs/licensing/spdx-policy.md` for headers. This guidance describes project policy;
it is not legal advice.
